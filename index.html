Cult Pixel Publisher

Cult Pixel Publisher is a private, single-user tool that uploads and schedules the videos of the Cult Pixel channel on YouTube. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and only acts on the channel it was authorized for.

It uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, schedule and thumbnails, and adds them to the channel's playlists. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
