Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Cult Pixel Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Cult Pixel YouTube channel to upload and schedule that channel's own videos.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to that channel, set their details and thumbnails, and manage the channel's playlists. It does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

Storage

The access token granted by Google is kept only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties. No analytics or cookies are used on this site.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
